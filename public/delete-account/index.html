Upstage POS – Account & Data Deletion Request

Official data deletion portal for Upstage POS (Application ID: com.myshopapp), developed by Upstage Technologies Private Limited.

1. Data Deletion Scope & Privacy Commitment

At Upstage Technologies, we respect your data privacy and right to complete data erasure. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated store data at any time, without needing to reinstall the mobile application.

Data that will be PERMANENTLY DELETED:

  • Merchant Profile & Authentication: Login credentials, phone number, email, passwords, owner name, profile photos, and store banner images.
  • Store Setup & Configuration: Shop display name, addresses, operational schedules, and custom settings.
  • Inventory & Products: All product listings, barcodes, category assignments, stock counts, and price label templates.
  • Staff & Role Accounts: All staff member credentials, waiter accounts, and kitchen display linkages.
  • Session & Token Data: Active Firebase Authentication sessions, device FCM push tokens, and local cache entries.
  • Cloud Storage Files: Product image uploads and store logo assets hosted in Firebase Storage.
⚖️ Legally Required Data Retention Notice (Statutory Compliance) In compliance with applicable Indian tax and accounting laws (including the Goods and Services Tax / GST Act, Income Tax Act, and Companies Act), completed commercial sales invoices, purchase records, and financial payment settlement logs (Razorpay / UPI) must be maintained for statutory audit purposes (minimum 7-year retention period). Upon account deletion, these records are permanently unlinked from your identity, stripped of personal credentials, and locked in an inaccessible, read-only compliance archive.
